About

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and healthcare management. I recently completed my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering at Central South University, advised by Prof. Hongyu Zhang, with a visiting year at the Department of Data Science and Statistics, National University of Singapore.

My research asks a practical question: how can deep learning be made useful — and trustworthy — in settings where a wrong answer carries a real cost? I have built models for Chinese medical named entity recognition, automatic ICD coding, medical insurance fraud detection, and long-horizon forecasting of industrial energy systems.

Much of this work was carried out on real data rather than on benchmarks: a provincial DRG payment reform covering tens of millions of claims and a healthcare fund in the tens of billions of yuan, where the deliverable was more than twenty reports handed to a provincial healthcare security administration.

Provincial2022 – 2024Key researcher

Coordinated DRG payment reform in the Chang-Zhu-Tan-Heng region, Hunan

Assessed how the reform affected payment efficiency, hospital management quality, and service delivery. Evaluated the accuracy of the underlying claims data, validated the DRG grouping scheme against clinical practice, and built the indicators used to monitor how the scheme performed after rollout.

Crawled multimodal review data from Qunar, cleaned and entity-aligned it, then modeled sentiment to build traveller profiles for itinerary recommendation.

Dataset of 5,000+ attractions and 7M+ reviews and travelogues · supported five SCI papers from the team

NSSFC2017 – 2020Key researcher

Multi-source intelligence fusion for emergency decision-making under big data

Built a hierarchical CNN–LSTM–MLP model that separates word–word relations from word–class relations, then validated it on 5,477 emergency news stories and 2,815 ordinary ones.

99.55% recognition and 94.82% classification accuracy
